Somobay Somiti is a comprehensive digital management app designed for members of savings societies, co-operatives, and samities. 

Access all your financial information securely in one place, stay up-to-date with your balances, and monitor your transactions transparently.

Key Features:
• Savings & DPS Tracking: Monitor your regular savings, recurring deposits, and active DPS accounts with full clarity.
• Loan Management: View active loans, upcoming installment dates, repayment schedules, and outstanding balances.
• Digital Passbook: Access your detailed digital passbook with complete transaction history and statement records.
• Instant Statements: Download or view your account statements anytime with real-time updates from your somiti.
• Safe & Secure: Protected access to ensure your financial data and personal details remain private and secure.
